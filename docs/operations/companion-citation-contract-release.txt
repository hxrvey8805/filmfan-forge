Companion citation contract — 2026-11-26

Problem and evidence
A controlled scene run failed receipt_count twice after the independent
reviewer approved its paragraphs. The raw failing receipts were not retained;
the available trace cannot distinguish an empty/oversized reference list from
overflow caused by splitting selected source ranges. Do not claim that subtype
was recovered from the live run.

Two concrete contract defects are reproduced locally:
1. The reviewer schema allowed supported=true with an empty support array,
   although the runtime correctly rejected that combination.
2. Two valid selected ranges could expand into ten <=700-character fragments
   and then be rejected against a limit intended for eight selected references.

Correction
- Use a nested schema union: approvals require nonempty exact references,
  issue=none and non-uncertain attribution. Rejections have their own route
  permitting empty references, so a refusal never requires fabricated proof.
- Retain the maximum eight model-selected references. Keep the original
  maximum eight fragments per selected range, each <=700 characters; bound
  the internal representation to 64 fragments overall. Preserve every selected
  original line. Do not truncate, add sources, repair quotations or accept an
  unknown source/range. Receipt retries reparse the original references rather
  than treating already-split fragments as extra model-selected references.
- All independent factual, identity, speaker, relevance and spoiler conditions
  remain in force. Source selection, full context, writer/reviewer prompts,
  model, reasoning and output-token settings are unchanged. No extra model
  calls or retries are introduced. Work/check revisions are updated.
