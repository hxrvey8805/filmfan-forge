Random Geek skin unlocks — 24 November 2026

Status: implemented and verified locally. Not deployed and not in TestFlight.

Product behaviour
- Monthly Pro can spend 1,500 earned XP on one random unowned Geek skin.
- The shop does not let users select a skin to purchase. The server rejects fresh skin:<specific> requests as well.
- Free retains the default Geek only. Annual includes all skins and has no random-skin shop.
- Existing owned skins are retained and can still be selected in the wardrobe.
- All 20 non-default skins are eligible, with equal probability among those the account does not own. Daily-spin unlocks are excluded from subsequent purchases too.
- The draw is immediately added to the wardrobe. No skipping, rerolling, selling or XP refund. Equipping is the user's choice.
- Confirmation shows the XP price before spending; the result reveals the actual granted skin. Probability details are collapsed under How it works.
- When every skin is owned, no XP can be spent. Insufficient XP also leaves the wallet unchanged.

Safety and recovery
- The server locks the wallet and commits the debit, random unlock and request receipt atomically.
- A persisted request ID survives navigation, remounts and uncertain transport results. Retrying returns the original skin, without a second draw or debit.
- Invalid success responses retain the request ID until a validated receipt is recovered.
- Completed historical specific-skin receipts remain readable; this does not permit another direct-skin purchase.
- The UI ignores a response after an account switch. Free/Annual attempts, cross-account receipts and concurrent final-skin attempts are covered by tests.
- Actor/director XP pack purchases and their existing price remain unchanged.
