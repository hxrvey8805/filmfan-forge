CineGeek spending safeguards — local candidate, 14 November 2026

Status
Implemented and tested locally. Not deployed, enabled or funded. No credit
purchases, live model requests, changes to credentials or extra AI spending.
Purchases remain disabled. The existing $5 lifetime private-beta ceiling is
unchanged. The staged monthly policy still defaults to disabled and zero money.

What is enforced when the monthly policy is activated
- A conservative money reservation BEFORE each provider request. It covers the
  entire input and the existing maximum completion/reasoning allowance.
- Whole-request USD limit across selection, drafting, review, repair, gateway
  retries and recap continuations. Limits captured for a request cannot grow
  when it resumes; a later policy reduction is also honoured.
- Account-wide UTC calendar-month cost limit, plus an optional UTC daily limit.
  Calls count in the month/day they start, even if the recap started earlier.
  Failed answers, abandoned requests, old beta calls and pack-funded questions
  all count. Renewing/changing a subscription does not erase spending.
- A configurable provider-call ceiling per request (staged default: 64). Even
  zero-cost provider attempts count. This is a loop guard, not a token reduction.
- The existing atomic $5 global ceiling still applies to every paid call.
- Denials persist in the database. A worker cannot catch a budget failure and
  save a partial answer or resume the same request with fresh limits.
- Failed/blocked requests do not use an included or purchased question. Their
  real provider costs remain on the money ledger. A new manual question/retry
  uses a new request ID but cannot evade the account or global money ceiling.
- A previously checked, safe stored answer requiring no new provider call can
  still be returned when the inference pool is exhausted.

Quality and safety
The model, provider pins, prices, reasoning, max_tokens, prompts, complete context,
source retrieval, writer/reviewer pipeline and spoiler/history checks are unchanged.
Budget exhaustion stops work; it never selects a cheaper model, truncates source
context, skips verification or returns an unreviewed draft. Availability may be
lower under tight limits. These controls cannot promise both an arbitrarily low
cost and completion of every possible recap/question.

Unknown bills and provider anomalies
Pending and uncertain requests retain their conservative reservation. The
existing transport halts further paid work after unknown costs, failed settlement,
unexpected routing or a bill larger than its reservation. No automatic refund,
clock reset, clearing of the halt or top-up is added. A provider billing above
the quoted/reserved bound is recorded truthfully and halted; the application
cannot guarantee an external provider will never overbill.
Reference: https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits

Saved-bill calibration (OFFLINE; not new answer-quality tests)
The numeric-only tests/fixtures/companion-billing.json contains 47 provider bills
from six completed historical question paths. It contains no prompts, answers,
captions, credentials or account IDs. Old actual bills are retained, including
paid retries and the previous cache-write costs; no estimated savings are needed
to make these accounting tests pass.

An illustrative $0.35 whole-request ceiling accepts all six paths. Their peak
conservative reservation plus already-spent money ranges from $0.209169 to
$0.321302; their actual total costs range from $0.072175 to $0.179912, rounded
up per call to integer micro-USD. Repeating the most expensive saved path 15
times costs $2.698680 and fits an illustrative $3 account-month ceiling.

Those example limits exist ONLY in tests. They are not approved/live launch
settings, a maximum cost for all titles, a representative average, or proof of
profitability. Fresh model outputs, long recaps, extra repairs, failed attempts,
funding fees, taxes, exchange rates, hosting and other services can change costs.
Do not promise all 15 questions AND paid top-ups under an unvalidated shared
per-account ceiling. Pack purchases must not merely bypass safeguards. Configure
adequate, priced funding and customer-facing limits before enabling pack sales.

Operator reporting
Service role has read-only access to two new reporting views:
  companion_spending_daily — known, unresolved and committed USD micro-units,
    provider call count and unknown bill count per user/UTC day.
  companion_spending_questions — the same costs per request, request status,
    original limits and any budget denial reason.
These views contain no conversation/source text. They are denied to PUBLIC,
anon and authenticated roles. No service-role budget funding privileges exist.
Include failed/released requests when measuring effective cost per delivered
answer; looking only at successful answers understates real business costs.

Read-only operator queries (database owner / existing SQL console)
  SELECT enabled,limit_micro_usd,committed_micro_usd,halted_reason
    FROM public.companion_sol_budget WHERE id='sol-beta';
  SELECT * FROM public.companion_usage_policy;
  SELECT day,sum(known_micro_usd) AS known_micro_usd,
    sum(unresolved_micro_usd) AS unresolved_micro_usd,
    sum(committed_micro_usd) AS committed_micro_usd,
    sum(provider_calls) AS provider_calls
    FROM public.companion_spending_daily GROUP BY day ORDER BY day DESC;
  SELECT status,budget_block_reason,count(*) AS requests,
    sum(committed_micro_usd) AS committed_micro_usd
    FROM public.companion_spending_questions GROUP BY status,budget_block_reason;

Release sequence
1. Keep the coordinated XP/tier release prerequisites in pro-xp-access.txt.
2. Apply 20261114120000_companion_spending_safeguards AFTER the staged monthly
   allowance and XP migrations. The migration does not enable the monthly policy.
3. Deploy the matching spoiler-free-companion error handling. Existing requests
   receive their original/current minimum cap on next begin/reserve. The original
   beta behaviour remains in effect while the monthly policy is disabled.
4. Keep purchases disabled until verified Apple entitlements and pack/refund
   handling are wired in. Validate candidate caps against the full supported
   question/recap workload before approving production values. A low monetary
   ceiling can stop work before all advertised questions are delivered.
5. Activate only with explicitly approved launch values. Do not raise the $5
   beta limit or the OpenRouter key limit as part of this migration. Any future
   public capacity/funding change is separate. For a temporary emergency stop,
   disable the global budget; do not disable the monthly policy and fall back
   to legacy beta accounting. If editing multiple policy/budget rows together,
   acquire the global budget row lock before the policy row, matching reservation.
6. Check known/unresolved spending and denial reasons after activation. Reconcile
   any unknown provider bill before clearing an existing global halt.
