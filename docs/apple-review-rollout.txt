What this adds
Monthly, Annual and Restore use the same purchase flow in onboarding and Profile.
Prices come from Apple; the annual monthly equivalent and full yearly charge are
both visible. Pending/cancelled/failed verification never completes onboarding.
An onboarding-save retry after successful verification does not repurchase.
Profile opens Apple's native Manage Subscriptions sheet even if the billing
server cannot be reached. Account switches cannot deliver someone else's receipt.

The new native adapter supports Release/TestFlight but accepts only Apple's
verified Sandbox app environment and the CineGeek bundle. It is explicitly
opted in; the real Production purchase opt-in remains absent. Debug test tools
and their separate Supabase project retain their existing gates.

A separate apple_review_* ledger in the live CineGeek project accepts Sandbox
receipts only for explicitly enrolled test accounts, for at most 30 days.
Enrollment alone gives no Pro access or questions. Normal accounts cannot enroll
themselves. Accounts with any existing Production entitlement or private Monthly
grant cannot enter this lane. Never enroll the owner's everyday Monthly account.
Verified test access is visible through the ordinary app plan/allowance RPCs and
ends when the receipt expires, is revoked, enrollment expires or review is disabled.
No test purchase writes the real paid ledger. Both plans remain 15/month; restoring
or switching plans does not reset usage. Existing XP, AI model, context, spoiler
checks and caps ($5 total, $0.35/question, $3/account/calendar month) are unchanged.
