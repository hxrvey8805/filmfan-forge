Changes
- Targeted speech/conversation/proposal summaries use the ordinary question
  route. Explicit chronological episode, series and time-range recaps retain
  their existing route and boundary rules.
- Valid source line ranges longer than one receipt are split at original line
  boundaries. No source text is truncated or rewritten. Each receipt stays at
  most 700 characters; the total stays at most eight per paragraph. Oversized
  single lines and legacy overlong quotations still fail closed. Independent
  identity, attribution, factual and spoiler review remains mandatory.
- At the user's express request, saved chat display is scoped to account,
  title and episode, independently of the current playhead. Each message keeps
  its original timestamp. Retired answers and evidence beyond a message's own
  original cutoff remain hidden. The server's timestamp-scoped safeHistory,
  reference resolution, source selection and new-question payload are unchanged.
