Library shelf rules and Home search — local changes, 22 November 2026

User-confirmed behaviour
- Watchlist cannot share a title with Watching, Watched or Favourites.
- Normal additions are blocked if the title is already in a conflicting shelf.
  They never automatically move/delete an existing title. The message explains
  that the viewer can remove it first or use the title's three-dot Move menu.
- Explicit moves between Watching and Watched replace the old status.
- Favourites may accompany Watching or Watched, or stand alone.
- My Lists remain independent; copies in other custom lists are untouched.
- Three-dot shelf actions retain compatible copies; Remove only deletes the selected copy.
- Home's top search opens Ask your Geek setup without adding/moving any library title.
  Each shelf's + button still adds to that shelf.
